Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy covers this website and Purrvival Town, the small app the Purrvival developer uses to publish Purrvival's own videos to Purrvival's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account, and to read how many people watched them. Purrvival Town is not offered to anyone else: the only accounts it connects to are ours.

This website

This website has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no ads. We don't collect anything about visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

What Purrvival Town accesses

Purrvival Town signs in only to Purrvival's own accounts, with permission we grant ourselves through each platform's official sign-in. It uses:

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploads our videos and reads our channel's subscriber count and our videos' views, likes and watch time.
  • Instagram (Instagram API): publishes our videos as Reels and reads our account ID, username and our Reels' views and likes.
  • TikTok (TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API): sends our videos to our TikTok account and reads our account's ID and display name and our videos' views and likes.

It never reads other people's accounts, messages, followers' personal data or comments.

How it's stored and shared

The sign-in tokens and the view counts are stored only on the developer's own computer. They are used only to publish our videos and to decide which videos to make next. We don't sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

Google and YouTube

Purrvival Town uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Purrvival Town's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Instagram and TikTok

Data from Instagram is also subject to the Meta Privacy Policy, and data from TikTok to the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time:

  • Google / YouTube: Google Account > Third-party apps & services
  • Instagram: Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services permissions

Once access is removed, Purrvival Town can no longer use that account, and we delete its stored tokens and statistics within 30 days. To ask us to delete anything we hold, email purrvival8@gmail.com; we'll do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

This website and Purrvival Town are not directed at children under 13 and collect no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Questions: purrvival8@gmail.com.
